about=PromptMap -- Open, Multi-Provider AI Cartography for QGIS. Edit your current QGIS canvas using generative AI. Render the live map view, write a prompt, and receive a GeoTIFF back — ready to use as a new layer. Supports FLUX.1 Kontext, FLUX 1.1 Ultra, FLUX.2 Editing (Black Forest Labs) and five Google Gemini image models (Nano Banana Pro, 2.1, 2, 2 Lite and original Nano Banana). Each result is georeferenced and documented with a GeoPackage containing model, prompt, timestamp and extent.
